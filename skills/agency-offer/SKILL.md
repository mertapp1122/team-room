# Writing an agency offer

## 1. Get the facts first
- Look the lead up in the CRM: `search_leads` with the company name, then `get_lead` with its id.
- Use only facts from the CRM and the conversation: goal, budget, current AI visibility, notes, who owns the lead.
- If the lead is not in the CRM, say so and ask for the basics (company, website, goal, budget). Never invent details.
- Leads in the demo CRM are fictional. Keep "(demo)" in their names.

## 2. Price
- Our packages start at $799/month and always include GEO, AEO and SEO together.
- Never quote below $799/month, never split the services to make it cheaper, and never offer a discount on your own.
- If their budget is under $799/month, say plainly that our packages start at $799/month and suggest a short call with the founder. Don't invent a cheaper option.
- Bigger scopes (many locations, many product lines, extra content) are quoted above $799/month after a call. Don't invent a number; write "custom quote after a call".

## 3. Shape of the offer
Write it ready to paste into an e-mail, in plain text, under about 250 words:

1. Subject line.
2. Where they are now: their goal and current AI visibility, in their words where possible.
3. What we do in the first 90 days, tailored to them:
   - GEO: get the brand named and cited by AI engines (entity facts, trusted sources, reviews).
   - AEO: clear answers to the questions buyers ask (FAQ and answer blocks, schema).
   - SEO: the pages that rank and that AI answers cite.
4. How we measure it: AI visibility (share of tracked AI answers that mention them), reported monthly.
5. Investment: from $799/month, GEO, AEO and SEO together.
6. Next step: one concrete action (for example a 30-minute call with the lead owner).

## 4. Tone and limits
- Plain, warm and confident. Short sentences. No hype words.
- Never promise rankings, positions or a specific number of AI mentions.
- End with a line for the teammate (not the client) listing anything you could not confirm from the CRM.
